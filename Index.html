      Navigate with your hand

      This interface can be controlled in mid‑air using your device's camera. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere.

      The camera feed is processed entirely on this device, inside your browser. It is never uploaded.

      There is no server, no account and no network connection carrying your video — so there is nothing to intercept.

      No images are stored. Each frame is analysed for hand position and discarded immediately.

      Only hand shape is detected. No faces are identified, matched or retained.

      You can revoke access at any time in your browser settings, and the interface still works by touch.
